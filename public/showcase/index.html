Feature Showcase

Welcome to the comprehensive demonstration of our custom Hugo shortcodes. These elements are designed to maintain the sleek, glassmorphism aesthetic of your site while providing rich content formatting options.

1. Interaction Alerts

Use these to highlight important information or warnings within your documentation or blog posts.

Attention: Ensure your config.toml structure matches the expected parameters for the partials to render correctly.

The grid shortcode allows you to create responsive layouts. It’s perfect for listing features, services, or project milestones.
